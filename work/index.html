Georgia Tech · 2017–2019 · Published workshop research

Language variation & evaluation

Studying where language models perform unevenly across communities and locations.

Social variation

Co-authored “Stylistic Variation in Social Media Part-of-Speech Tagging.” Found correlations between network structure and tagging errors; the mixture-of-experts approach did not improve tagging performance.

Geographic variation

Applied the Hilbert-Schmidt Independence Criterion to lexical and geographic variation in Lyon Twitter data. Presented at the NeurIPS 2018 Black in AI Workshop.

Why it informs my work

Evaluation should examine variation across groups and settings, alongside aggregate metrics. Negative experimental results are part of the evidence.

MIT CSAIL · Summer 2015 · Research internship

Egyptian Arabic ASR

Low-resource speech recognition under James Glass.

Question

Would diacritized pronunciation dictionaries improve word error rate on a 10-hour Egyptian Arabic speech corpus?

Contribution

Built pronunciation dictionaries and experiments using Kaldi, C++, and Bash; compared GMM-HMM and DNN acoustic-model approaches.

Finding

Grapheme-based lexica outperformed diacritized approaches in this low-resource setting.
