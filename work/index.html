Egyptian Arabic ASR - MIT CSAIL

Research (2015)

Low-resource speech recognition for Egyptian Arabic using Kaldi acoustic models.

Problem

Egyptian Arabic is a low-resource dialect with limited transcribed training data and complex phonological variation. Standard ASR approaches from Modern Standard Arabic didn't transfer cleanly.

Built

Kaldi-based speech recognition pipeline. Built pronunciation dictionaries for Egyptian Arabic. Compared grapheme-based lexicon approaches against diacritized methods. Experimented with GMM-HMM and DNN acoustic models.

Technologies

Kaldi C++ Bash GMM-HMM DNN acoustic models Egyptian Arabic

Why it matters

Low-resource language work requires understanding the signal at a linguistic level, not just tuning hyperparameters. Grapheme lexicon outperformed diacritized approach. Research under James Glass at MIT CSAIL.

Geolinguistic Analysis (HSIC / Lyon)

Published Research (NeurIPS 2018)

Statistical analysis of dialectal variation in Lyon French tweets using Hilbert-Schmidt Independence Criterion.

Problem

Language varies geographically, but measuring that variation in a statistically principled way is hard. Standard approaches rely on hand-labeled dialects.

Built

Applied HSIC (kernel independence test) to measure correlations between lexical choices and geographic location in Lyon Twitter data. Identified spatial linguistic signals without dialect labels.

Technologies

Python HSIC kernel methods Twitter data geolinguistics Lyon French

Why it matters

Principled statistical approach to linguistic geography. Published at NeurIPS 2018 Black in AI Workshop. Under Jacob Eisenstein at Georgia Tech.
