Stylistic Variation in Social Media Part-of-Speech Tagging

Research posterMIT CSAIL · 2015

Lexical Modeling of Egyptian Arabic for Automatic Speech Recognition

Taha Merghani, Tuka Al Hanai, and James Glass

We compared pronunciation dictionaries and acoustic models using 10 hours of training speech. With our best acoustic model, the dictionary based on normalized spelling produced slightly fewer word errors than the automatically diacritized dictionary.

02 / PhD research interests

What does an evaluation miss?

I first approached this through language variation. Working in retail gave me a different view of the same problem. When inventory records diverged from what was on the shelves, experienced workers knew how to recover, and much of that work went unrecorded.

Who experiences the errors?

I’m interested in measuring how errors and their consequences vary across groups, especially when average performance looks acceptable.

Who gets to intervene?

I want to study when people can correct or contest an AI-assisted decision, and how their interventions change the outcomes we observe.
