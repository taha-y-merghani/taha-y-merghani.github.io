About Taha Merghani

I grew up in Sudan, studied computer engineering at Jackson State University, and earned an M.S. in computer science at Georgia Tech. With Jacob Eisenstein, I studied how language use varies across social networks and geography, and what that variation means for natural language processing.

As an undergraduate researcher at MIT CSAIL, I worked on Egyptian Arabic speech recognition. At Apple, I built tools to help Siri engineers trace failures across languages. Both experiences made me attentive to the choices that determine which errors a team can see.

My research interests are algorithmic fairness and human judgment in AI-assisted decisions. I’m particularly interested in what happens when the people who see an error and the people with the authority to correct it are different.
