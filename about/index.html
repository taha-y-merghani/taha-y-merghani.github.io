About me

A language researcher
with a builder’s instinct.

I’m Taha Merghani, an applied AI and NLP engineer based in New Jersey, looking to join a team in New York City or work remotely.

I began in language technology before the current LLM wave: Egyptian Arabic speech recognition at MIT CSAIL, multilingual debugging tools at Apple Siri, and research on language variation at Georgia Tech. Since then, I’ve worked on customer analytics pipelines, enterprise computer vision, and local voice interfaces.

I’m drawn to the point where a promising model meets a real workflow: the awkward data, the integration that breaks, the person who needs the result to make sense. I enjoy making those problems concrete and building through them.

I grew up in Sudan and studied computer engineering at Jackson State University before earning my M.S. in Computer Science at Georgia Tech. Moving between languages and institutions has shaped what I notice about technology—and who it works for.

I also write. Technical essays help me make my reasoning clear; personal essays give the work its wider context.
